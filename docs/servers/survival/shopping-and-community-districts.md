# Shopping and Community Districts

<Persona who="ned">
  The districts belong to everyone. Build something your neighbours will be glad to see next door.
</Persona>

Survival has two shared areas near spawn: the Shopping District, where players run shops, and the Community District, where players build things everyone can use.

## The Shopping District

### Setting up a shop

Shops in the Shopping District are built on plots. Each plot is linked to its shop, so the [District Warden](./district-warden.md) can keep track of it. For how chest shops work, see [Shops & Economy](./shops.md).

### Shop rules

- One theme per shop. Each shop sells one type of item or service, like redstone, wood, or enchanted books. No variety shops.
- Shops only. No bases, farms or other non-shop builds in the district.
- All shops go inside the district. Shops built elsewhere may be removed.
- Don't block access to your neighbours' shops.
- Friendly competition is welcome. Two shops can sell the same thing.

### Be honest with customers

> The Lord detests lying lips, but he delights in people who are trustworthy. (Proverbs 12:22)

- Your shop signs must match what you actually sell, and at the price shown.
- No misleading or scam shops.
- Giveaways and donation or trash chests are fine. Running a shop that sells items for nothing isn't, because it undercuts everyone else.

### Keep your shop stocked

If your shop has been mostly sold out for more than 60 days, the District Warden may flag it to Staff. A flag isn't an automatic removal. Staff look at each flagged shop and decide what to do, and they may contact you first.

Going on a break? Stock up before you go, or let Staff know.

## The Community District

The Community District is for builds that benefit everyone, like:

- Community farms.
- Arenas and minigames.
- Donation centres.
- Trading halls.

> Carry each other's burdens. (Galatians 6:2)

### Community District rules

- Everything here must be open to the public.
- Claiming your build is still encouraged. Use trust levels so others can use it without being able to break it.
- Every automated farm needs an on/off switch.
- Build farms to keep lag down. See [Farms and Lag](./farms-and-lag.md).

For more detail, see [The Community District](./community-district.md).
