# Building on Survival

<Persona who="ned">
  Good neighbours ask first. A quick message before you build next door saves a lot of trouble later.
</Persona>

Survival is one shared world, so how you build affects everyone around you. Here's how to build big and stay a good neighbour.

> Each of you should look not only to your own interests, but also to the interests of others. (Philippians 2:4)

## Where you can build

- Almost anywhere in the world is open for your base.
- No bases in the spawn chunks, or directly outside the spawn area. Community builds there are welcome.
- Shops go in the Shopping District, and public builds go in the Community District. See [Shopping and Community Districts](./shopping-and-community-districts.md).

## Be a good neighbour

- Building near someone? Message them first to check they don't have plans for the area.
- Clearly mark your planned builds, so others know the space is taken.
- Clearly mark who owns your base, farm or build, with a sign or a name.

If someone builds right up against your claim, or you can't agree on who should have an area, talk to a Staff Member. Staff will look at each situation case by case and help work it out, and that can include asking someone to move their build.

## Claim your stuff

Claiming your land protects it from griefing and theft. You earn claim blocks as you play. See [Claim Protection](./claim-protection.md) for how it works.

If you're inactive for more than 90 days, your claim expires and the land is no longer protected. There's no warning before this happens, so if you're taking a break, log in at least once every 90 days to keep your claim.

## Stealing and griefing

Taking or wrecking what isn't yours is never okay. That includes:

- Taking items from someone's chests, farms or base.
- Breaking, changing or covering someone else's build.
- Killing someone's pets or farm animals.

This goes for unclaimed builds too. No claim doesn't mean free for all.

> You shall not steal. (Exodus 20:15)

## Pranks vs. griefing

Pranks between friends are fine when everyone is in on it, like filling a friend's house with chickens. It becomes griefing when:

- The other person didn't agree to it, or isn't your friend.
- Something gets damaged or lost.
- They've asked you to stop.

If you've been griefed, don't fix it yourself. Report it with /report. Staff can check what happened and roll back the damage.

## Keep builds suitable for everyone

No obscene or questionable language, names or builds. Survival is played by all ages.

Planning something that might be borderline, or that needs context, like a historical or Bible scene? Check with Staff before you build it.

## The world seed

Don't try to find out or share the world seed. It keeps exploring, finding structures and hunting for resources fair for everyone.

## AFK players

Senior Staff may remove players who are AFK for a long time. It isn't a punishment. An idle player keeps chunks loaded and slows the server down.
