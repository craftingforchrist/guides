# Gamemodes

<Persona who="finn">
  Figure out how you win, then go win it.
</Persona>

Mixed runs on [PGM](https://pgm.dev), which supports dozens of gamemodes. Each map tells you its objective - check `/map` at the start of a round - and many maps are *mixed*, combining more than one of the modes below in a single match.

You won't see every one of these in rotation, but here's what each gamemode is and how you win it.

## Objective Gamemodes

These are team-versus-team rounds built around a goal on the map. Most matches you play will be one of these.

| Gamemode | How you win |
| --- | --- |
| **Destroy the Monument (DTM)** | Break the enemy team's monument block(s) while protecting your own. |
| **Destroy the Core (DTC)** | Break through to the enemy core and make its lava leak out the bottom. |
| **Capture the Wool (CTW)** | Take wool from the enemy side and place it on your team's victory monument. |
| **Capture the Flag (CTF)** | Grab the enemy flag and return it to your base to score. |
| **King of the Flag (KOTF)** | Hold a central flag for your team instead of capturing an enemy's. |
| **Flag Football (FFB)** | Carry the flag into the enemy goal, football-style. |
| **Control the Point (CP)** | Capture and hold control points, which earn your team points every second. |
| **5 Control Points (5CP)** | Control-point play across a line of five points, pushing toward the enemy's end. |
| **King of the Hill (KOTH)** | Capture and hold the hill; the first team to the score target wins. |
| **Attack / Defend (A/D)** | One team attacks a set of objectives against the clock; the other defends. |
| **Payload** | Escort a payload cart into enemy territory while they try to stop it. |
| **Race for Wool (RFW)** | Race the other team to collect and place your wools first. |
| **Bridge** | Carry your wool through the enemy portal before they do the same to you. |

## Elimination Gamemodes

In these, staying alive matters. Run out of lives and you're out.

| Gamemode | How you win |
| --- | --- |
| **Blitz** | Everyone has limited lives; you're eliminated when they run out. Last team or player standing wins. |
| **Blitz: Rage (BR)** | Blitz, but every hit is lethal - one clean strike eliminates. |
| **Bed Wars** | Destroy enemy beds to stop them respawning, then wipe out the bed-less teams. |
| **Skywars** | Fight across floating islands with no respawns; last one standing wins. |
| **Survival Games (SG)** | Loot chests for gear, then outlast everyone else. |
| **Infection** | Survivors try to outlast the infected; the infected try to turn everyone. |

## Combat & Score Gamemodes

Fast rounds decided by kills or points rather than a fixed objective.

| Gamemode | How you win |
| --- | --- |
| **Deathmatch (TDM)** | Teams rack up kills; the team at the kill or score limit (or ahead on time) wins. |
| **Free for All (FFA)** | No teams - every player is on their own. |
| **Rage** | One-hit-kill combat; melee drops opponents instantly. |
| **Scorebox** | Earn points by bringing items to, or standing in, your team's scorebox. |

## Special Formats

| Gamemode | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Mixed** | A map that combines several of the objective types above in one round - the format the server is named after. |
| **Arcade** | A catch-all for unconventional, novelty maps that don't fit the standard modes. |

:::tip Not sure what the objective is?

Run `/map` for the current map's details and `/proximity` to see how close each objective is to being completed. The [Command Reference](./commands.md) covers both.

:::
