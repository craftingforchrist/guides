# Accounts and Profiles

<Persona who="mabel">
  Your account is yours. Keep your password to yourself, and never hand your login to anyone, even a friend.
</Persona>

Your username, skin and profile are the first thing other players see. Here's what keeps them suitable, and what keeps you and others safe.

> The Lord detests lying lips, but he delights in people who are trustworthy. (Proverbs 12:22)

## Usernames, skins and profiles

Everything about how you appear needs to be suitable for all ages. That includes:

- Your Minecraft username and skin.
- Your Discord username, avatar, banner and status.
- Your website profile, if you have one.

That means no swearing, nothing rude or suggestive, and nothing that mocks or targets others. If it's borderline, it's probably not okay.

If your username or skin isn't appropriate, you'll be banned until you change it. Once it's fixed, you're welcome back.

## Your Discord name

On our Discord, your name must match your in-game name, or a nickname most of the community knows you by. This helps Staff and players know who's who.

## Don't pretend to be someone else

Impersonating other players or Staff isn't allowed. That includes copying someone's name or skin to trick people, or claiming to be Staff when you're not.

If someone is impersonating you, contact a Staff Member.

## No scamming

Scamming is stealing through tricks or lies, like:

- Promising to pay for something and not paying.
- Trading something that isn't what you said it was.
- Asking for items for a "giveaway" that never happens.
- Fake "free rank" or "free Nitro" offers.

On Survival, shop signs also have to match what the shop really sells. See [Shopping and Community Districts](../servers/survival/shopping-and-community-districts.md).

## Be honest with Staff

Don't lie to Staff, whether it's in a report, an appeal or when they ask you what happened. Being honest, even about something you got wrong, always goes better.

## One account, one player

If you've been punished, don't come back on another account. Ban evasion can lead to all your accounts and your IP address being banned, which is much harder to undo.

If you think your punishment was unfair, appeal it instead.

## Sharing an account

Some players share an account with a sibling or a friend. That's your choice, but you're responsible for everything that happens on it. If someone else breaks the rules on your account and it gets banned, the ban stays on the account, no matter who was playing.

If a shared account is banned and someone who uses it starts playing on a different account, that may look like ban evasion. It may be investigated, and it could lead to a ban. If this happens to you, open a ticket and explain before you log in on another account. See [Punishments and Appeals](./punishments-and-appeals.md).
