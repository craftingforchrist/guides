# Respect and Harassment

<Persona who="theo">
  If something someone says doesn't sit right with you, you don't have to just put up with it. Tell a Staff Member.
</Persona>

Crafting For Christ is for everyone: kids, teens and adults, from all over the world. Treating each other with respect is the rule that matters most, and it applies in-game, on the website and on Discord.

> Do not let any unwholesome talk come out of your mouths, but only what is helpful for building others up according to their needs. (Ephesians 4:29)

## What this rule covers

These are never okay, anywhere on the Network:

- Harassment or bullying: picking on someone, ganging up on them, or following them around to upset them.
- Threats of any kind, including joking threats.
- Discrimination: treating someone badly because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, gender, disability, age or religion.
- Hate speech, slurs, racism, sexism and homophobia.
- Being disrespectful to Staff. You can disagree with a decision, but do it politely and through a ticket.

## Different beliefs

We're a Christian community, but not everyone here believes the same things, and that's okay. You don't have to agree with someone, but you do have to treat them with respect. Talking about faith is welcome. Mocking someone for what they believe is not.

## Banter vs. bullying

Friendly teasing between friends is fine when everyone is enjoying it. It becomes harassment when:

- The other person has asked you to stop.
- It keeps happening to the same person.
- Other people join in against one person.
- It's about who someone is, not something they did in the game.

If you're not sure, stop. Staff will judge by how it lands, not by what you meant.

## If it's happening to you

1. Don't reply in kind. Arguing back usually makes it worse.
2. Use /ignore to stop their private messages, or block them on Discord, so you don't see their messages.
3. Report it. Use /report in-game, or open a ticket on Discord if you want to hear back.
4. If it happened in a voice channel, leave the channel and report it straight away. Voice channels aren't recorded, so tell us what happened as soon as you can.

Please report it privately rather than calling it out in chat. That gives everyone a fair hearing.

## What happens next

A Staff Member checks the chat logs and decides what to do. Punishments depend on how serious it was and the player's history. Some things, like hate speech and threats, are treated as serious straight away. See [Punishments and Appeals](./punishments-and-appeals.md).
