# Chat Guidelines

<Persona who="theo">
  Good chat is chat everyone can join in. Keep it kind, keep it clean, and keep it readable.
</Persona>

Chat is where most of the community happens, so a few simple guidelines keep it a good place to be for players of every age.

> Whatever is true, whatever is noble, whatever is right, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is admirable, think about such things. (Philippians 4:8)

## Keep it clean

- No swearing or cursing. That includes censored or misspelled swear words, and abbreviations of them. Mild words like heck or frick aren't counted as swearing, but Staff may ask you to tone it down.
- No rudeness. Disagreeing is fine. Insulting people is not.
- Nothing inappropriate. If you wouldn't say it in front of a younger player's parents, don't say it in chat.

The chat filter catches a lot, but not everything. Getting around the filter, for example with spaces, symbols or misspellings, counts as filter circumvention and is treated the same as saying the word.

## Don't spam

Spam makes chat impossible to read for everyone else. Spam means:

- Sending lots of messages in a short time.
- Sending the same message over and over.
- Sending very long messages, or walls of text.

This also covers flooding chat with symbols, emojis or capital letters.

## English only

All chat needs to be in English. We have players from all over the world, and it means everyone can follow the conversation and Staff can keep chat safe. If you need help with something and English is hard for you, open a ticket and we'll do our best.

## Stay on topic on Discord

Each Discord channel has a purpose. Keep conversations in the right channel, and put low-quality or unrelated posts in #off-topic.

## Keep personal details private

<Persona who="mabel">
  Your real name, address, school and phone number stay with you. Nobody in chat needs them.
</Persona>

Never share your own personal details, or anyone else's, in chat. If someone asks for them, don't answer, and tell a Staff Member. For more on staying safe online, see [Online Safety Do's and Don'ts](../safeserver/online-safety.md) in Safe Server.

## If someone breaks these guidelines

Don't call them out in chat. Use /report in-game, or open a ticket on Discord if you want to hear back. See [Safety Tools](../safeserver/safety-tools.md).

If you break them yourself, Staff decide what happens case by case. A first slip is usually a quiet word or a warning. Repeated or deliberate offences can lead to a mute or a ban. See [Punishments and Appeals](./punishments-and-appeals.md).
