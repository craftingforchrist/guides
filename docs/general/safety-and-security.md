# Safety and Security

<Persona who="mabel">
  Someone's real-life details are never yours to share. Not as a joke, not as a threat, not ever.
</Persona>

These rules protect the people in our community and the servers we all play on. They're some of the most serious rules we have.

## Doxxing

Doxxing means finding or sharing someone's real-life personal information without their permission, like their:

- Real name, address or school.
- Phone number or email.
- Photos, or social media accounts they haven't shared.
- IP address or location.

Doxxing, threatening to dox someone, or posting anything that supports it is never allowed. It's treated as one of the most serious offences on the Network.

If someone has your details or is threatening to share them, don't reply. Tell a Staff Member straight away, or email support@craftingforchrist.net. If you're a younger player, tell a parent or trusted adult too.

## DDOS and attacks on the Network

A DDOS (or DOS) attack tries to knock a server or person offline by flooding it with traffic. Attacking the Network, or threatening to, is never allowed. That also goes for attacking or threatening other players.

On Discord, DDOS threats also break Discord's Terms of Service.

## Don't lag the servers on purpose

The servers are shared by everyone, so don't do things that slow them down for others, like:

- Unreasonable redstone clocks.
- Excessive TNT explosions.
- Building up huge numbers of entities, like mobs, items or minecarts.

Accidents happen, and Staff will usually talk to you first. Doing it on purpose is a different matter. See [Farms and Lag](../servers/survival/farms-and-lag.md) for how to build big without causing lag.

## Discord's age limit

Discord requires everyone to be at least 13. We'll remove any account that belongs to someone younger. Using Discord under 13, even with a parent's permission, breaks Discord's Terms of Service and may get the account suspended.

There's no minimum age to play on our Minecraft servers. Younger players can still reach Staff any time with /report in-game, without needing Discord.

## If something feels wrong

If anyone makes you feel unsafe, asks for your personal details, or threatens you or the Network:

1. Don't reply or argue.
2. Take a screenshot if you can.
3. Report it with /report, a Discord ticket, or support@craftingforchrist.net.

See [Safety Tools](../safeserver/safety-tools.md).
