# Voice Chat Safety

<Persona who="theo">
  If a voice chat stops feeling okay, you can always just leave. Then tell a Staff Member what happened.
</Persona>

Voice channels on our Discord are a great way to build, play and hang out together. Here's what to expect, and how to keep them comfortable for everyone.

## How voice channels work

- Voice channels aren't recorded.
- Staff aren't always listening. If a Staff Member is around, they may drop in, especially after a report.
- That means it's up to everyone in the channel to keep it friendly, and to report anything that isn't.

Voice works best with people you already know and trust.

## Discord Clips

We don't record voice channels, but Discord has a Clips feature that can let someone in the channel save a short recording that includes your voice. If you'd rather that didn't happen, go to User Settings, then Data & Privacy, and turn off Allow my voice to be recorded in Clips.


## What's expected in voice

- No mic spamming, like loud noises, screaming or holding down a soundboard.
- No playing music or videos unless everyone in the channel is okay with it.
- No inappropriate audio, language or music.
- If someone asks you to stop doing something, stop.
- No harassing, insulting, provoking, upsetting or threatening anyone.

The same rules apply in voice as everywhere else. See [Respect and Harassment](./respect-and-harassment.md).

## Keep your details private

It's easy to say more out loud than you'd ever type. Don't share your real name, school, address or anything that shows where you live, and don't ask others for theirs.

## If something goes wrong

1. Leave the channel. You don't have to explain or say goodbye.
2. Report it as soon as you can. Use /report in-game, open a Discord ticket, or message a Staff Member. Since voice isn't recorded, tell us who was there, roughly when it happened, and what was said.
3. If you can, write down what happened while it's fresh.
4. Block the person on Discord if you don't want to hear from them again.

If someone made you feel unsafe, tell a parent or trusted adult too.
