# Punishments & Appeals

<Persona who="mabel">
  Owning up early is always the shorter road.
</Persona>

**Overview:**
Most players never need this page. But if you've been punished, or you're worried about what would happen if you slipped up, it's fair enough to want to know how it works.

The short version: we decide punishments case by case. There's no fixed list where a particular offence always earns a particular penalty. This page is the long version of what our [Network Rules](https://craftingforchrist.net/rules) say about punishments.

### How We Decide

When Staff look at an incident, they weigh up:

* **How serious it was.** Swearing once in frustration and deliberately targeting someone with hate speech are not the same thing, even though both come under the same rule.
* **Whether it was deliberate.** Accidents and misunderstandings happen, especially with newer players.
* **Your history on the Network.** A first slip after two years of playing well reads very differently to the fourth incident this month.
* **What happened afterwards.** Owning it and putting it right counts for a lot. So does the opposite.

This means outcomes vary, and that's intentional. It lets us be gentle where gentleness is right and firm where it isn't.

:::info "But someone else got off lighter"

Possibly - they may have had a different history, or the context may have been different. We can't discuss another player's punishment with you, in the same way we wouldn't discuss yours with them.

:::

### Following Staff Directions

If a Staff Member asks you to do something, like stop an argument, move a build or leave a channel, please do it. They're trying to keep things running well for everyone.

If you think a direction was wrong, follow it first, then raise it afterwards by email at support@craftingforchrist.net or through a Discord ticket. Arguing in chat won't change the decision, and it often makes things harder for you.

### Being Honest with Staff

Don't lie to Staff, whether in a report, an appeal, or when they ask what happened. Owning up, even when it doesn't look good, always goes better than being caught out later.

### What Punishments Look Like

Depending on severity, an outcome might be:

* A quiet word from a Staff Member
* A formal warning
* A mute, for chat-related issues
* A temporary ban
* A permanent ban

Some things sit at the serious end almost regardless of context - hate speech, doxxing, threats, anything that puts a younger player at risk, and attacks on the Network itself.

:::note The Senior Staff Team's discretion

Beyond the case-by-case approach above, the Senior Staff Team keeps the right to ban or unban any player from any of our services, for any reason, without notice. It's rarely used and never lightly, but it exists so the team can act quickly when the community's safety calls for it.

:::

### Griefing and Rollbacks

If you've been griefed, Staff can usually inspect what happened and roll the damage back. Report it rather than repairing it yourself - rebuilding over the top makes the logs harder to read.

A prank between friends isn't griefing, and we don't treat it as one. Rearranging someone's chest room when you know they'll laugh is fine. What matters is whether the person on the receiving end is in on it - if you have to guess, ask them first.

The rollback and the punishment are separate. Getting your base restored doesn't mean nothing happened to the person responsible; we just don't discuss the details with you.

### Ban Evasion

Coming back on another account while you're banned is treated seriously. It can result in all your accounts and your IP being banned, which is much harder to undo than the original punishment.

If your ban felt unfair, appeal it. That door is genuinely open, and it's a far better outcome than the alternative.

### Shared Accounts

If you share an account with a sibling or a friend, you're responsible for everything done on it. A ban stays on the account, whoever was playing at the time.

If a shared account is banned and someone who uses it starts playing on another account, it may be investigated as ban evasion. Open a ticket and explain before you do.

### How to Appeal

You can appeal any punishment.

1. Go to [craftingforchrist.net/appeal](https://craftingforchrist.net/appeal). That's the quickest route, and it goes straight to the right people.
2. If you can't reach the appeal form, open a ticket on our [Discord](https://craftingforchrist.net/discord) with `/support create`, or email support@craftingforchrist.net.
3. Tell us your in-game name, roughly when it happened, and what you'd like us to look at again.
4. Include anything relevant - screenshots, context, or simply your side of it.

**What helps:**

* Being straight about what happened, including the parts that don't flatter you
* Explaining context we might have missed
* Giving it a little time - appeals are read by a person, not a bot

**What doesn't:**

* Making the same appeal repeatedly
* Arguing about it in public chat
* Coming back on another account while you wait

### If You Think a Staff Member Got It Wrong

Staff are people, and people make mistakes. If you believe you've been wrongly punished, or that a Staff Member has misused their position, we want to hear about it.

Email support@craftingforchrist.net or open a ticket on our [Discord](https://craftingforchrist.net/discord) with whatever evidence or details you have. It goes to the Senior Staff Team.

Please raise it that way rather than in public chat. It gets a fairer hearing, and it's kinder to everyone involved - including you, if it turns out you were right.

### Reporting: /report or a Ticket?

There are two ways to report a player:

* `/report` in-game is the fastest. It goes straight to Staff, even if you don't have Discord. You won't get a reply, but Staff do see every report.
* A Discord ticket takes a little longer, but you can add screenshots and you'll hear back.

Our Staff are spread across time zones, so a report might be picked up within the hour, or it might wait until morning. For step-by-step instructions, see [Safety Tools](../safeserver/safety-tools.md).

Please report privately, rather than calling it out in public chat. Public accusations rarely help, and they can be very unfair to someone who turns out to have done nothing wrong.

### Senior Staff Decisions

The Senior Staff Team may ban or unban any player from any Crafting For Christ service, for any reason, without notice. This is rarely needed, but it lets us act quickly when something serious happens. Senior Staff decisions can still be appealed. They go through the normal appeal process and are reviewed by an Administrator.

### Complaints About the Owner

If your complaint is about the owner of Crafting For Christ, there's a separate process. The owner is left out of it completely, and an independent third party is brought in to review the complaint.

To raise a complaint about the owner, email support@craftingforchrist.net or safeserver@craftingforchrist.net. Say in your email that it's a complaint about the owner, so it goes to the right people.

### When the Rules Change

The rules may change from time to time, and it's up to you to keep checking the [rules page](https://craftingforchrist.net/rules). After a change, there's a one week grace period before it's enforced.
