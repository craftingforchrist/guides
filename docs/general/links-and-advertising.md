# Links and Advertising

<Persona who="mabel">
  Not every link is what it says it is. If you don't know where it goes, don't click it, and don't share it.
</Persona>

Sharing links can be really helpful, like a build tutorial, a YouTube video of your base, or a Bible reading plan. But links can also lead somewhere unsafe, so here's what's okay and what isn't.

## Links that are fine

- Crafting For Christ pages, like the website, forums, knowledge base and Discord.
- Helpful, family-friendly content, like Minecraft tutorials, the Minecraft Wiki, or your own YouTube or Twitch videos and streams of your builds.
- Bible verses and Christian resources.

## Links that aren't allowed

- Anything NSFW or inappropriate, or anything that leads to it.
- Downloads from places you don't trust, like "free" clients, hacks or resource packs from unknown sites.
- Links that ask you to log in or enter your details, like fake "free Nitro" or "free Minecraft" sites. These are scams.
- Shortened or disguised links that hide where they really go.

If a link turns out to be inappropriate, it doesn't matter that you didn't check it first. Please check before you share.

## Advertising

Don't advertise other communities. That means:

- No other Minecraft server addresses.
- No Discord invites to servers that aren't part of Crafting For Christ.
- No promoting your own server or paid service, unless Staff have said it's okay. Sharing your own videos or streams of your builds is fine.

This keeps the community focused, and it protects younger players from being drawn into spaces we can't keep safe.

## Churches and pastoral care

We're a Christian community, so sharing an online church service or a pastoral care service can be okay within reason. Ask a Staff Member before posting, and they'll let you know.

## Not sure?

Ask a Staff Member before you post. It's always better to check first.

If someone sends you a link that looks wrong, don't click it. Use /report in-game, or open a ticket on Discord. See [Safety Tools](../safeserver/safety-tools.md).
